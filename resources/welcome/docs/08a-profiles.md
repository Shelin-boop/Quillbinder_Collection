A **theme** is only a look: colors and fonts. A **profile** keeps everything else: your options, toolbars, window size and place, panels, keyboard set, backup settings, standard colors and saved layouts, and it names its theme. Switching the profile changes all of that at once.
**Try it:** choose **View > Profiles > Save Settings as New Profile…**, give it a name, change a few things (a theme, a toolbar), and switch back to **Default** under **View > Profiles**.
- The profile **Default** is where your settings are kept at first. It cannot be deleted; the other profiles can.
- **View > Profiles > Manage Profiles…** renames, copies, deletes, exports and imports profiles. An exported profile is one file you can take to another computer; folders in it that do not exist there are asked about.
- **View > Toolbars > Profiles** shows a small bar with the profile in use. **View > Toolbars > Labels** shows the label colors of the open project, one click each.
- **File > Options > Appearance > Standard Colors** sets the labels that new projects start with and the colors of the text color and highlight palettes. They belong to the profile.
